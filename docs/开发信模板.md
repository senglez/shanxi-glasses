# 开发信与 LinkedIn 话术模板

使用说明：
- 方括号 `[ ]` 里的内容要换成真实信息，发之前通读一遍。
- **每封信都放一个具体产品页的链接**，不要只放首页，回复率差别很大。
- 一次只发给一个人，不要群发抄送。每天 20–50 封为宜，太多容易被当成垃圾邮件。
- 标题控制在 6–8 个英文单词，不要用 "Hot sale"、"Best price"、"Dear Sir/Madam" 这类词。
- 两次跟进之间隔 3–5 个工作日。三次都没回复就停，不要一直发。

在哪里找到联系人：
- **ImportYeti.com**（免费）：搜 perfume bottle、glass jar、glassware，看哪些美国公司在从中国进口，以及它们的进口频率。
- **LinkedIn**：搜职位 Founder、Purchasing Manager、Sourcing Manager、Product Development，加上行业词 perfume、candle、skincare、homeware。
- **Google**：搜 `"niche perfume" brand [国家]`、`candle company [城市]`、`glassware wholesaler [国家]`，到官网的 Contact 或 About 页面找邮箱。

## 模板 A：香水品牌（小众香水、私人定制香水）

**标题选项**
- `Heavy-base perfume bottles for [Brand]`
- `15 mm crimp bottles + pumps, low MOQ`
- `Air-bubble perfume bottles for your next launch`

```
Hi [First name],

I came across [Brand] and liked [具体到某一款香水或某个系列].

We source heavy-base glass perfume bottles from factories in Shanxi, China,
including clear, air-bubble, polished and spray-coated designs. Most use a
15 mm crimp neck and we supply the bottle, pump, collar and cap as a tested set.

Stock molds start from about 3,000 pcs, so you can use your own color and logo
without paying for a new mold.

Here is one design that could suit your style:
[产品链接，例如 https://www.shanxiglassworks.com/products/perfume-bottles/air-bubble-glass-perfume-bottle-90-ml/]

Would it help if I sent 2–3 sample bottles that match your current size?

Best regards,
[Your name]
[Company] | WhatsApp: [+number]
https://www.shanxiglassworks.com/
```

## 跟进邮件

**第 1 次跟进（第一封发出 3–5 个工作日后，直接回复原邮件）**
```
Hi [First name],

Just following up on my note below. If useful, I can send a short PDF with
our [perfume bottle / candle jar / glassware] options and MOQs.

Best,
[Your name]
```

**第 2 次跟进（再隔 5 个工作日，给一个具体的新信息）**
```
Hi [First name],

One more idea: [一个新的具体点，例如 "we just added a faceted 100 ml bottle
that works with standard 15 mm pumps"]: [产品链接]

If now isn't the right time, no problem. Just let me know and I won't follow up again.

Best,
[Your name]
```
